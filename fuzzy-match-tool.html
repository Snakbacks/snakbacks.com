Free online fuzzy match tool

Paste two lists and match each messy value to the closest name in a clean list, despite typos, capitals, punctuation and word order. No sign-up.

  • Runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded
  • Up to 10,000 lines per list
  • Same engine as our Google Sheets add-on

Copy a column from Google Sheets or Excel and paste it in. If you paste several columns, only the first is used. Blank lines are kept so the results line up with your rows.

Higher means stricter. At 0.8 a value needs a score of 0.8 or more to match; anything less stays blank instead of guessing. Try 0.7 to catch more, or 0.9 for fewer wrong matches.

How the matching works

Both lists are cleaned first: capitals, accents and punctuation are ignored, so "ACME Pty. Ltd" and "Acme Pty Ltd" are treated as the same. Exact matches are found straight away. For everything else, each value gets a similarity score from 0 (nothing alike) to 1 (identical) against every name in the clean list, and the best one wins if it reaches your threshold.

The score is the highest of three measures: Jaro-Winkler similarity on the whole text (good at typos like "Inytech" and "Initech"), the same on the words sorted alphabetically (so "Corp Umbrella" matches "Umbrella Corp"), and how many whole words the two share.

To keep big lists fast, names of very different lengths are skipped at high thresholds, exactly as in the add-on. So "Globex" only matches "Globex Corporation" at a threshold of 0.65 or lower; the tool tells you when that happens.

Check the borderline matches

No threshold makes fuzzy matching perfect. Two different companies can have similar names, and a short name can sit close to several others. Sort by score and look over anything between your threshold and about 0.9 before you rely on it, especially for invoices or customer records. Rows marked "No match" show the closest candidate in grey, so you can see whether lowering the threshold would help.

Is my data private?

Yes. The matching runs as JavaScript in this page, on your device. Your lists are never sent to us or anyone else, and this site has no tracking or analytics. You can check by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads: it still works.
