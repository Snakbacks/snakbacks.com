Duplicate Remover

Free on the Marketplace

Find repeated rows in Google Sheets on the columns you choose. Review them highlighted, mark them in a column, or copy them to a new sheet. Menu based, no sidebar.

Install from the MarketplaceRead the how-to guide

Why not use Sheets' built-in Remove duplicates?

Google Sheets' own Data > Data cleanup > Remove duplicates deletes rows straight away. You don't see what went, and a stray space can stop real duplicates from being caught. Duplicate Remover never deletes anything. It shows you the duplicates first (highlighted, labelled, or copied to a new tab), ignores capitals and extra spaces, and lets you decide what to remove. Use the built-in tool for quick, throwaway clean-ups, and Duplicate Remover when you want to check before you delete.

What it does

Highlight duplicate rows

Colours every repeat light red so you can review them first. The first occurrence stays unhighlighted.

Add a "Duplicate?" column

Writes Unique or Duplicate next to your data, ready to filter or sort.

Copy duplicates to a new sheet

Copies every repeat to a new tab and leaves your original data unchanged.

Clear highlights

Removes only the colour the add-on added.

How to use it

  1. Select the columns

    Click the column letters that define a duplicate, for example Name and Email. To compare whole rows, click any single cell.

  2. Pick an action

    Open Extensions > Duplicate Remover and choose highlight, mark, or copy.

  3. Review the result

    A message tells you how many duplicate rows were found and which columns were compared.

Try it first with Extensions > Duplicate Remover > Insert sample data. Row 1 is treated as the header, capitals and extra spaces are ignored, and empty rows are never counted as duplicates.

Narrow access. It asks for one permission, to the spreadsheet you use it in. Your data never leaves Google Sheets. See the privacy policy.
