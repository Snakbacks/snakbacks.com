One lifetime licence that unlocks Pro features in the Snakbacks add-ons for Google Sheets. No subscription.

What you get in Fuzzy Match Pro

Fuzzy join

Bring columns from another sheet into this one, matched by similar text, with a match score. Select the key column, then choose Extensions > Fuzzy Match > Fuzzy join selected columns (Pro). Results go to a new sheet, and your data is not changed.

FUZZYMATCHN

The top candidate matches for each value, with scores, so you can review doubtful matches yourself.

FUZZYMATCH, FUZZYLOOKUP and FUZZYSCORE stay free.

How to activate

  1. Install Fuzzy Match

    Free from the Marketplace. Pro needs version 4 or later, which updates automatically.

  2. Buy the licence

    Pay on Gumroad. Your licence key arrives by email within a few minutes.

  3. Enter and activate

    Extensions > Fuzzy Match > Enter Pro key, paste your email and key, then Extensions > Fuzzy Match > Activate Pro.

Pro is activated per spreadsheet, so repeat the last step in each spreadsheet you use it in. A licence is for your own use and tied to the email you bought with.

Questions

Does Pro cover Duplicate Remover?

Pro features for Duplicate Remover are planned, and your licence will cover them at no extra cost when they ship. Please buy only if Fuzzy Match Pro is worth it to you on its own.

My key will not activate

Email info@snakbacks.com and we will sort it out.

Can I use templates instead of the add-on?

Yes. The Fuzzy Match Template (US$9) and Data Cleanup Kit (US$15) work without installing anything.
