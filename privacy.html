Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Snakbacks ("we") publishes add-ons for Google Sheets™: Fuzzy Match and Duplicate Remover. This policy explains what data they touch and what we do with it. In short, your spreadsheet data stays in your spreadsheet.

What the add-ons access

  • Only the spreadsheet you use them in. The add-ons ask for one permission, to view and manage the spreadsheet they are installed in. They read and write that spreadsheet only when you run a menu item or use their formulas, for example to match text, mark or copy duplicate rows, or add an example or help tab. They cannot see your other files.
  • Your Snakbacks Pro key, if you buy Pro. It is checked inside the add-on, with no connection to any server, and stored in a hidden sheet in that spreadsheet.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share the contents of your spreadsheets. The add-ons make no external requests and we have no servers that receive your spreadsheet data.

If you buy a product, Gumroad processes the payment and gives us your email address and purchase details, so we can email your licence key and provide support. We keep a record of each sale (email address, product and date) for that purpose only. Gumroad's privacy policy covers that data: gumroad.com/privacy.

Google user data

The add-ons' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the add-ons' features to you. We never transfer it to third parties, use it for advertising, or let humans read it, except with your explicit consent for support, or where the law requires.

Removing your data

Uninstalling an add-on removes its access to your account. To remove Pro from a spreadsheet, delete the "Snakbacks Pro" tab and the hidden "Snakbacks licence" sheet, or contact us.
